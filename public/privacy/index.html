Privacy Policy – Pants on Fire! Find the Liar

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how Martin Richardson, trading as Hikaru Tech (“we”, “us”), handles information in the Pants on Fire! Find the Liar app and at pantsonfire.app. Contact: jubbamhc@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details (optional): email, display name, and a securely hashed password if you create an account.
  • Gameplay: nicknames, answers, votes, and statistics (games played, wins, etc.).
  • Text chat: messages you send in online lobbies are relayed to other players in that lobby.
  • Voice chat: if you enable microphone access, your voice is streamed live to players in your lobby via LiveKit. We do not record or store voice audio.
  • Moderation: if you report another player, we store the report reason, optional note, lobby code, player identifiers, timestamp, and recent lobby chat lines to investigate abuse.
  • Blocks: if you block someone while signed in, we store the block on your account; blocks on device are also kept locally.
  • Purchases: records of theme packs and tiers you unlock. Payments are handled by Google Play and Stripe; we never see card details. RevenueCat helps confirm mobile purchases.
  • Bug reports (optional): description, screenshot, and basic device/app diagnostics you choose to send.
  • Technical: a random app installation ID and standard server logs (e.g. IP address) for security and reliability.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or Advertising ID, and the app contains no ads or third-party analytics.

How we use it

To run the game and online lobbies, manage accounts, unlock purchases, investigate reports, fix bugs, and prevent abuse. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Service providers who process data on our behalf: hosting (Railway, IONOS), database/auth (Supabase and our game server database), voice (LiveKit), purchases (RevenueCat, Google Play, Stripe). Other players see your nickname, chat, and voice in lobbies you join.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Lobby chat on the server is kept only briefly to support abuse reports. Moderation reports are kept for up to 24 months then deleted or anonymised unless we must keep them longer for legal reasons.

Delete your account in the app via Settings → Delete account, or visit Delete account, or email jubbamhc@gmail.com from your account email. We remove account, profile, and entitlement rows on our servers. Purchase records with Google Play, Stripe, or RevenueCat remain with those providers as legally required.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data, or complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Online chat and voice are with other players; please play with people you know. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above.
